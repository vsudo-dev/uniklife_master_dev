# Troubleshooting: Login Issues

---

## Cannot Log In — Wrong Password

1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login page
2. Enter your email
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder)
4. Click the reset link
5. Enter a new password

> Reset links expire after 1 hour. Request a new one if expired.

---

## Logged In but Seeing Wrong Dashboard

**Symptom:** You expected to see the tenant dashboard but see the platform dashboard, or vice versa.

**Fix:**
1. Sign out
2. Sign in again
3. If the issue persists, your role may have been changed — contact the Tenant Admin

---

## "Access Denied" After Login

**Cause:** Your account exists but has not been assigned a role or branch.

**Fix:**
1. Contact the **Tenant Admin**
2. They need to assign your role (Practitioner / Staff) on the Staff page
3. If you need branch access, they must assign you to the branch

---

## Logged In but Cannot See Any Clients

**Cause:** You are a Practitioner but have not been assigned to any branch.

**Fix:**
1. Contact the Tenant Admin
2. Ask them to assign you to the relevant branch on the Staff page

---

## Session Expired — Keeps Redirecting to Login

**Cause:** Your login session has expired (usually after a long period of inactivity).

**Fix:** Simply log in again. Sessions are persistent once active.

---

## Email Not Receiving Reset Link

| Check | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Spam / Junk folder | Check it |
| Correct email address | Confirm you used the right email |
| Email delay | Wait 2–3 minutes and check again |
| Still not arriving | Contact the Super Admin — they can manually reset via the platform |

---

## New Staff Member Cannot Find Signup Page

New staff sign up at the main UNIKLIFE URL.  
After signup, the Tenant Admin assigns their role on the Staff page.  
Until the role is assigned, they will see an empty dashboard.
