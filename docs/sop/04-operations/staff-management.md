# Staff Management

---

## What Is Staff Management?

Tenant Admins can manage their team — practitioners, staff, and coordinators — from the Staff page.

---

## Staff Page

Shows all staff members for the tenant.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Name | Staff member's full name |
| Email | Login email |
| Job Title | Their role title (e.g. "Senior Nutritionist") |
| Branch | Which branch they are assigned to |
| Specialisation | Area of expertise |
| Status | Active / Inactive |
| Hire Date | When they joined |

---

## Adding a New Staff Member

1. The staff member must first **sign up** using the UNIKLIFE URL
2. After signup, their account is created
3. The Tenant Admin opens the Staff page
4. Locates the new user and assigns:
   - Job Title
   - Branch
   - Role (Practitioner / Staff)
   - Specialisation
5. Save

---

## Editing a Staff Profile

1. Go to Staff page
2. Click the **Edit** icon on the staff row
3. An edit form appears inline below the row
4. Update the fields
5. Click **Save**

Fields that can be edited:
- Job Title
- Branch assignment
- Specialisation
- Hire Date
- Status (Active / Inactive)

---

## Deactivating a Staff Member

Change their status to **Inactive**.  
- They can no longer log in
- Their historical records (appointments, notes) are preserved

---

## Branch Assignment

Staff can be assigned to one branch at a time from this page.  
For practitioners needing multi-branch access, use the branch assignment feature in Tenant Settings.

---

## Who Can Manage Staff?

| Role | Can Add | Can Edit | Can Deactivate |
|------|:-------:|:--------:|:--------------:|
| Super Admin | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Tenant Admin | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Practitioner | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
