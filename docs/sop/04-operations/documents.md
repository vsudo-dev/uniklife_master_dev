# Documents

---

## Where Do Documents Come From?

Documents are stored in **MyAppz CRM** against each contact.  
UNIKLIFE displays them on the client profile — it does not store documents itself.

---

## Viewing Documents

1. Open the client's profile
2. Go to the **Documents** tab (if available for this tenant's modules)
3. Documents uploaded to this contact in MyAppz are listed here

---

## What Types of Documents Are Supported?

Whatever is attached to the contact in MyAppz:
- PDFs (reports, prescriptions, consent forms)
- Images (before/after photos, lab reports)
- Word documents

---

## Uploading Documents

Documents are uploaded in **MyAppz** against the contact.  
UNIKLIFE reads and displays them — uploading directly in UNIKLIFE is not currently supported.

> To upload a document → go to MyAppz → find the contact → attach the file.  
> It will appear in UNIKLIFE automatically.

---

## Document Privacy

Documents are stored in MyAppz's storage.  
Only staff with access to that contact in MyAppz can see them.  
In UNIKLIFE, the same access rules apply — Tenant Admin and Practitioner can view; Staff cannot access health documents.

---

## Important Notes

- Documents tab only appears if the **Documents module** is enabled for the tenant
- If the client's contact is not synced to UNIKLIFE, their documents won't be accessible from the profile
- Document storage quota is managed in MyAppz, not UNIKLIFE
